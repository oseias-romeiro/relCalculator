About RelCalculator

RelCalculator is an interactive tool created to simplify the study of relations between sets. Originally developed to support linear algebra and discrete mathematics exercises, the project evolved into a user-friendly platform that helps students and enthusiasts analyze relation properties, visualize matrices, and better understand concepts such as reflexivity, symmetry, antisymmetry, and transitivity through intuitive calculations and visual representations.
